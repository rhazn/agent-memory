# Working on Writing

When editing prose to satisfy lint rules, preserve Philip's exact wording, voice,
emphasis, and sentence structure wherever possible. Make the minimum required
change per alert and audit every final diff change for necessity.

This preference concerns lint fixes. It does not prohibit substantive editing
when Philip requests it.

Vary sentence length and read paragraphs for rhythm. Philip dislikes uniformly
short sentences with a repetitive, staccato cadence. Keep related reasoning
together instead of splitting every clause to satisfy a prose-lint heuristic.
Prefer reasonably short, readable sentences by default, not uniformly short
sentences or a hard word limit. The ASD-STE100 recommendation for direct harness
communication does not apply to authored prose unless Philip explicitly requests
that specialized style for the deliverable.
When shortening an article, rebuild coherent paragraphs rather than leave
disconnected fragments. Preserve the argument, including explanations that need
a longer sentence, without adding irrelevant caveats.

Do not add dates or timestamps without explicitly telling Philip. Omit incidental
verification dates from operating documentation.

Do not use defensive prose to document a decision the agent should challenge.
Surface conflicting evidence before acting, recommend the supported choice, and
resolve the decision in conversation. Documentation should explain how to operate
the system, not protect the agent or make Philip look forgetful. Omit statements
that merely explain the obvious or disclaim guarantees nobody requested.
