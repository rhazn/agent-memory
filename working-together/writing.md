# Working on Writing

When editing prose to satisfy lint rules, preserve Philip's exact wording, voice,
emphasis, and sentence structure wherever possible. Make the minimum required
change per alert and audit every final diff change for necessity.

This preference concerns lint fixes. It does not prohibit substantive editing
when Philip requests it.

Vary sentence length and read paragraphs for rhythm. Philip dislikes uniformly
short sentences with a repetitive, staccato cadence. Keep related reasoning
together instead of splitting every clause to satisfy a prose-lint heuristic.
When shortening an article, rebuild coherent paragraphs rather than leave
disconnected fragments. Preserve the argument, including explanations that need
a longer sentence, without adding irrelevant caveats.
