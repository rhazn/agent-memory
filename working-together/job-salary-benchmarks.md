# Paris salary benchmarks for Philip's job search

Research date: 11 November 2026.

## Scope

Full-time employment in Paris. Figures are EUR gross annual base salary,
excluding bonus and equity unless stated otherwise.

Benchmark the job title and level, not Philip's combined biography. Do not add
an automatic premium for the PhD, technical background, or basic AI skills.

These bands are rounded research-based estimates, not measured percentile
distributions. Most studies combine Paris and Ile-de-France.

## Role benchmarks

| Role | Annual base salary | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Senior Software Engineer / Senior Product Engineer | EUR 55-75k | Product companies can pay EUR 75-90k+, but that is not the general baseline. Includes applied-AI features. |
| Mid-level Product Manager | EUR 52-65k | Established individual-contributor PM. Includes data and AI products. |
| Senior Technical Product Manager | EUR 65-80k | Senior PM is the comparison; direct evidence for the technical variant is weaker. No assumed technical or AI premium. |
| Engineering Manager | EUR 65-95k | Team-level manager, not Head of Engineering. Team size and management scope matter. |
| Early-career ML Engineer / Data Scientist | EUR 42-65k | Data science tends toward EUR 42-52k; ML engineering toward EUR 48-65k. |
| Postdoctoral Researcher | Approximately EUR 35-43k | Indicative public-research range. Institution, funding and experience determine actual pay. |

## Evidence

### Senior Software Engineer / Senior Product Engineer

- Silkhom 2026: senior full-stack JavaScript developers in Paris,
  EUR 55-70k base.
- TPC 2026: Paris senior medians of EUR 59k full-stack and EUR 64k backend,
  including variable pay.
- Individual employer budgets can be higher. These demonstrate upside, not
  the typical market.

### Product management

- Lity: Paris confirmed PM, EUR 52-65k base; senior PM, EUR 65-79k base.
- TPC: Paris PM with 3-5 years of experience, EUR 59k median, including
  variable pay.
- Senior Technical PM uses senior PM as a proxy, without an invented
  technical premium.

### Engineering Manager

- GetPro: Paris EM with 2-5 years in management, EUR 65-85k base; senior EM,
  EUR 80-100k base.
- Silkhom: confirmed Paris EM, EUR 80-95k base.
- Differences between sources reinforce the importance of scope.

### Early-career ML Engineer / Data Scientist

- Silkhom: junior Paris data scientist, EUR 42-52k base.
- Silkhom AI/ML: junior Paris ML engineer, EUR 48-65k base.

### Postdoctoral Researcher

- Paris Dauphine-PSL AI postdoc: approximately EUR 3,200 gross/month,
  equivalent to EUR 38.4k annually.
- Paris CNRS postdoc example: EUR 3,571.35/month, approximately EUR 42.9k
  annually.
- Earlier Inria examples outside Paris paid approximately EUR 33.5-35.1k
  annually. These are contextual evidence, not a complete Paris benchmark.

## How the job-search agent must use this

- Use these benchmarks to give salary context when discussing roles.
- Never use them as salary floors, eligibility criteria, or reasons to exclude
  or hide opportunities.
- Do not treat them as Philip's stated salary expectations or minimum.
- Published employer pay remains separate from the market benchmark. Never
  present an estimated band as the vacancy's published pay.
- The agent may use the relevant benchmark when suggesting answers to
  salary-expectation questions.
- Whenever suggesting such an answer, remind Philip of the usual salary band,
  its pay basis, and why the proposed answer sits within or outside it.
- Keep suggested answers as drafts. Do not submit them.
- Distinguish base salary, bonus, equity, and total compensation.
- These benchmarks concern full-time Paris roles. Do not assume that they
  change Philip's standing hours or location preferences.
- Refresh dated evidence before relying on it for a consequential salary
  recommendation.
