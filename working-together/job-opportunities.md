## Confirmed Preferences

- Ideally, find part-time employment under a French CDI. Full-time is acceptable.
- Prefer CDI, but CDD is also acceptable, including research and postdoc roles.
- Paris in-person or hybrid work is acceptable. Remote work is acceptable when
  the employer permits working from Paris, France. No regular office attendance
  outside Paris. Do not silently expand the search to other locations or assume
  that an Ile-de-France label establishes a suitable office location.
- Seek technical product management, software engineering, R&D, researcher,
  postdoc, and research-adjacent positions. Data engineering, open data, data
  science, and AI-related work are also exciting directions.
- Give extra weight to work with data and data-driven products. Philip is
  particularly interested in turning data exploration into useful product
  features, not only maintaining data infrastructure.
- Inria is a particularly attractive employer. Keep suitable Inria research
  engineering and related roles in the search. A decision not to apply to one
  vacancy for unrelated reasons is not negative feedback on this direction.
- Target intermediate and more experienced roles appropriate to Philip's
  professional background. Assess research roles by their responsibilities and
  eligibility, not by industry seniority labels alone.
- Calibrate product-management seniority separately from overall engineering
  and research experience. Philip considers Staff-level product roles a stretch
  given his product experience. Do not make them default shortlist recommendations;
  prefer roles with a closer match to his demonstrated product responsibilities.
  Keep unusually relevant Staff product roles clearly labelled as stretch options.
- Lower-entry roles are also acceptable in data engineering, open data, data
  science, and AI. Do not apply the usual intermediate-plus threshold to these
  tracks. Gaining substantive AI experience for the CV is a positive goal.
- Give extra weight to technical product management roles in AI, including AI
  inference: they build on Philip's product experience while adding substantive
  AI experience to his CV. Keep the other role tracks in the search.
- Prefer opportunities where the PhD opens doors or provides a meaningful
  advantage. It is not a requirement for every suitable role.
- Exclude community-building, developer relations, sales, sales-adjacent work,
  and outbound outreach responsibilities. Philip prefers limited interaction
  rather than responsibility for cultivating a community or partner pipeline.
  Do not reject a partnership title automatically: translating inbound partner
  needs into technical specifications and engineering delivery can be a very
  good fit when another team owns the inbound funnel and relationship sourcing.
- French is A2. English is very good. Favor roles compatible with these skills.
  Exclude mandatory French fluency or another explicitly required level above
  A2. Distinguish optional French from required French and flag unclear working
  languages, especially for product stakeholder work or teaching duties.

## Application Drafts and Cover Letters

- Inspect the application questions before choosing the draft format. When the
  employer asks substantive questions, draft answers to those questions. Follow
  the exact prompts and word or character limits. If the form also includes a
  cover-letter field, draft a tailored cover letter as well, even if optional.
  Application questions do not replace a separate cover letter. Otherwise,
  include a tailored cover-letter draft when there are no substantive questions.
- The greeting, paragraph structure, and sign-off rules below apply to cover
  letters, not individual application-form answers.
- Start every cover letter with a greeting. Address the hiring manager by
  verified name when available; otherwise address the team.
- Use the job listing's language, except use English for French-language jobs.
- Use basic, colloquial language rather than formal application boilerplate.
  Aim for three to four short body paragraphs, with room to develop the motivation
  rather than compress it into a brief opening. Add substance, not filler.
- End every draft with "Best," followed by "Philip Heltweg" on the next line.
- Focus on why this specific company and vacancy interest Philip: what excites
  him, why he wants this work, and how it connects to his interests and background.
  Mention verified experience where it explains that motivation and hints at fit.
  Do not recap the CV or make proving his qualifications the main purpose. Ground
  the motivation in the actual vacancy; do not invent a history of admiring a company.
- Explain why he is interested. For part-time opportunities, say that he likes
  to be flexible. Do not imply that a full-time vacancy offers part-time work.
- Mention Paris and international-team fit when useful. Do not routinely
  highlight limited French when French is not required.
- Ask for feedback on the drafts as well as the opportunities. Improve reusable
  guidance here through the same memory-learning process.

- Keep the established search process without a mandatory extra exploratory pass.
  When an unfamiliar role type emerges during a search and looks relevant, mention
  it as a possible direction. Explain how its responsibilities connect to Philip's
  background and which role names could help him describe and find that work.
  Do not silently add it to the standing search criteria.
- Surface published pay bands directly in each opportunity's summary. Include
  the currency and pay period, and distinguish full-time-equivalent pay from
  actual part-time pay. State when pay is not published; do not invent a range.
- Retrieve current CV evidence through [Philip's background](../ABOUT.md).
  Do not invent expertise, salary expectations, availability, or eligibility.
- Philip's Java experience is older, and he has no GPU-scale infrastructure
  experience. Do not treat these as current strengths.
- Philip does not currently have strong SQL skills. He has some Python
  experience and limited PyTorch experience. Keep otherwise suitable roles
  requiring SQL or PyTorch in the search: he is willing to learn and prepare,
  including getting familiar with PyTorch before applying. State the gaps
  accurately; do not claim established expertise in application drafts.
- Infrastructure as a service is not Philip's area of expertise, including IaaS
  storage products. Do not infer this expertise from general infrastructure,
  software engineering, or data-pipeline work. Deprioritize roles that require
  established IaaS product expertise.
- Background checks are not a concern for Philip. Mention a required check as
  a factual condition when useful, not as a reason to lower a role's ranking.
- Deprioritize roles requiring existing expertise or independent ownership of
  regulatory approvals. Consider an otherwise attractive role as a stretch,
  with the gap stated clearly.
- Verify employment filters each time. The same portals are used for agency
  searches and may retain incompatible freelance-only preferences.
- Check actual contract terms, hours, language requirements, and remote-country
  eligibility in the full vacancy. Do not infer part-time availability or an
  English-speaking workplace from a title or listing language.
- Before recommending a vacancy, open its Apply link and inspect the follow-up
  page and any visible application questions for additional requirements,
  particularly mandatory languages. Opening a form is allowed; do not submit,
  upload a CV, populate fields, or trigger an application or external contact.
  If inspecting the questions requires such an action, stop and flag the gap.
- Keep personal employment criteria separate from
  [agency engagement criteria](business-development.md). Do not inherit agency
  engagement signals or agency-specific interview exclusions.
- Read and maintain the personal-job section of
  [opportunity search history](opportunity-search-history.md) to avoid repeats.
- Store reusable preferences and feedback here. Except for the explicitly
  requested known exclusions above, keep individual vacancy identifiers only in
  that bounded record, not application histories or a search-run archive.
  Follow `agent-memory` for candidate and confirmed learnings
  when feedback provides meaningful new guidance.
